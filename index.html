Schuylkill County guilty plea

Action News reported on April 8, 2026 that Joe Ellis of Ellis Building Works LLC entered a guilty plea in Schuylkill County in 2025 for making a “False Statement to Induce Agreement for Home Improvement Services.”

Pennsylvania's Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act defines “home improvement fraud” and includes, among other conduct, making a false or misleading statement with fraudulent intent to induce a person to enter a home-improvement agreement.

PENNSYLVANIA REGISTRATION CONTEXT

What Pennsylvania requires contractors to disclose

Pennsylvania's Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act requires a home-improvement contractor registration applicant to disclose whether the applicant has been convicted of certain offenses related to home improvement, fraud, theft, deception, or fraudulent business practices, and whether the applicant or covered businesses received certain final civil judgments related to home-improvement transactions within the preceding ten years.

This archive does not currently have Joseph Ellis's underlying registration application. The statute is included only to explain the disclosure framework. No statement is made here about what Ellis reported to the Commonwealth.

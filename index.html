PENNSYLVANIA REGISTRATION CONTEXT

What Pennsylvania requires contractors to disclose

Pennsylvania's Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act requires a home-improvement contractor registration applicant to disclose whether the applicant has been convicted of certain offenses related to home improvement, fraud, theft, deception, or fraudulent business practices, and whether the applicant or covered businesses received certain final civil judgments related to home-improvement transactions within the preceding ten years.

This archive does not currently have Joseph Ellis's underlying registration application. The statute is included only to explain the disclosure framework. No statement is made here about what Ellis reported to the Commonwealth.

METHODOLOGY

Evidence labels used on this site

Confirmed / adjudicated

A court disposition, judgment, statute, or official record supports the statement. Docket numbers are provided so readers can independently verify the entry.

Reliably reported

A named news organization reports the fact, but the underlying court document has not yet been reproduced here.

Allegation

A consumer, litigant, or reviewer made the statement. It is not presented as established fact.

Pending verification

A case or lead has been identified, but disposition, identity, or other material details remain unresolved.

Corrections policy

This archive is intended to be factual and updateable. If a source document shows that an item is inaccurate, satisfied, reversed, dismissed, expunged, or otherwise materially changed, the entry should be corrected promptly and the source retained where lawful for historical context.

SOURCE DOCUMENT POLICY

Original documents & privacy

Preferred publication method

For Pennsylvania dockets, this archive should provide the docket number, a link to the official UJS case-search portal, and—when useful—a redacted copy of the preserved docket. Redaction protects unnecessary personal information while preserving the disposition, charge, judgment amount, dates, and parties needed for verification.

Do not republish sensitive fields

Before posting a docket PDF, remove or obscure full dates of birth, home addresses of private plaintiffs or unrelated persons, OTN/incident identifiers where unnecessary, private phone numbers, financial information, signatures, children's information, and any confidential or sealed material.

Removal / update rule

If a record later becomes sealed, expunged, subject to limited access, vacated, reversed, satisfied, or materially corrected, review the hosted copy promptly and update or remove it as appropriate. The official court record remains the source of truth.

PENNSYLVANIA REGISTRATION CONTEXT

What Pennsylvania requires contractors to disclose

Pennsylvania's Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act requires a home-improvement contractor registration applicant to disclose certain convictions related to home improvement, fraud, theft, deception, or fraudulent business practices, as well as certain final civil judgments related to home-improvement transactions.

This archive does not currently have Joseph Ellis's underlying registration application. The statute is included only to explain the disclosure framework. No statement is made here about what Ellis reported to the Commonwealth.

METHODOLOGY

Evidence labels used on this site

Confirmed / adjudicated

A court disposition, judgment, statute, or official record supports the statement. Docket numbers are provided so readers can independently verify it.

Reliably reported

A named news organization reports the fact, but the underlying court document has not yet been reproduced here.

Allegation

A consumer, litigant, or reviewer made the statement. It is not presented as established fact.

Pending verification

A case or lead has been identified, but disposition, identity, or other material details remain unresolved.

Corrections policy

SOURCE DOCUMENT POLICY

Original documents & privacy

Preferred publication method

For Pennsylvania dockets, this archive provides docket numbers, links to the official UJS case-search system, and may provide redacted copies of preserved public docket records.

Redaction protects unnecessary personal information while preserving the disposition, charge, judgment amount, dates, and parties necessary for verification.

Sensitive information

Before publishing court documents, unnecessary personal identifiers should be removed or obscured, including full dates of birth, private residential addresses, financial information, signatures, children's information, and confidential or sealed material.

Removal / update rule

If a record later becomes sealed, expunged, subject to limited access, vacated, reversed, satisfied, or materially corrected, the hosted copy and accompanying description should be reviewed promptly.

The official court record remains the source of truth.

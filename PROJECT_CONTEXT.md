## Editorial and privacy rules

Classify statements as confirmed/adjudicated, reliably reported, consumer allegation,
or pending verification. Do not use editorial labels such as “scammer” or “fraudster,”
treat complaints as adjudicated facts, attribute same-name cases without evidence, or
publish the publisher's identity, personal email, or unnecessary sensitive identifiers.

Only reviewed and appropriately redacted copies of actual public docket records may be
published. Never fabricate a PDF or link to a file that does not exist.
